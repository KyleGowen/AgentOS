# People And Collaboration

Useful context about recurring collaborators, stakeholder groups, and communication preferences.

Use roles, first names, or private aliases when that is enough. Avoid unnecessary personal details.

## General Rules

- Store collaboration context only when it helps future work.
- Prefer roles and stakeholder groups for work context.
- First names are acceptable when they materially help Kyle's workflow.
- Private aliases are acceptable when they reduce unnecessary identifying detail.
- Do not store sensitive personal information.

## Stakeholder Groups

### Customer Success

- Often needs help translating technical behavior into customer-facing implications.
- For work involving reporting, data correctness, or customer support, be clear, source-backed, and careful.

### Non-Engineering AI Coaching Participants

- Often arrive with vague workflow ideas or spreadsheet/document tasks.
- Good help means building enough with them that they understand the next version themselves.

### Engineering Teammates

- Kyle collaborates asynchronously across time zones.
- Good handoffs should include context, current state, risks, and the next concrete action.

## Key Collaborators

- Add first-name or private-alias notes only when they recur and materially improve future collaboration.
