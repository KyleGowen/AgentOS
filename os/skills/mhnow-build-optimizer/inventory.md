## Capture rule

When Kyle states or provides clear screenshot evidence that he owns an armor piece or weapon, persist it here without interviewing him for the rest of his inventory. Armor-screen numbers are recorded as displayed defense, not inferred grade/level. Faded/ghosted item cards are treated as not owned unless other evidence establishes ownership.

## Recommendation behavior

When Palico recommends a build, check this inventory, clearly mark owned gear versus target/unowned gear, and prefer practical owned-gear paths when the DPS tradeoff is small. A tracked build in `saved-builds.md` is not by itself proof of ownership.
