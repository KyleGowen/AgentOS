## Capture rule
When Kyle states or provides clear screenshot evidence that he owns an armor piece or weapon, persist it here without interviewing him for the rest of his inventory. Armor-screen numbers are displayed defense, not inferred grade/level. Faded/ghosted item cards are treated as not owned unless other evidence establishes ownership. Kyle supplied a complete armor-list sweep on 2026-08-27; this file is the baseline snapshot and should be incrementally updated when he reports forging or upgrading gear. Ask for current armor status only when freshness materially affects a recommendation; do not routinely re-interview him.

## Recommendation behavior
When Palico recommends a build, check this inventory, clearly mark owned gear versus target/unowned gear, and prefer practical owned-gear paths when the DPS tradeoff is small. A tracked build is not by itself proof of ownership.
